# 📚 Curated Free Resources

Every resource here is **free** (or has a free tier sufficient to learn). Quality over quantity — these are the ones I'd recommend to a friend.

> Want to add one? Open a PR. Criteria: free to access, structured (course/playlist/book — not a single random video), and the author actually knows the topic.
